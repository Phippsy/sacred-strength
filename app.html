Principles

What the app should feel like

1

The check-in is the centre

A few seconds a day: how you feel, your energy, your mood, a short diary note. Everything else follows from it.

2

Works with or without a cycle

Irregular, unknown or changing cycles are welcome. "I'm not sure" is a fine answer.

3

Heather does the listening

The app collects the signals. Heather reads them and responds personally.

4

Suggestions, never rules

Every session has a gentler option. Some women want a set plan, others want to choose. Ask which.

5

Ask for very little

No 55-screen sign-up. Collect only what helps, share nothing with advertisers, and say so plainly.

6

Permission to rest

Rest days count. A low-energy day is information, not failure.

Two ways to plan

Guided by the cycle, or guided by feelings

The cycle is a way into body awareness, not the goal. Women can move between the two as things change.

Cycle-guided

When the cycle is known and fairly regular

  • Sessions are placed around estimated phases
  • The daily check-in can soften or swap a session
  • Logging a period re-plans the week automatically
  • A best guess is fine to start; it gets better as she logs
Feelings-guided

When it isn't: irregular, unknown, changing or on hormonal contraception

  • No dates needed; the daily check-in shapes the options
  • A diary of how she feels, energy and symptoms
  • More one-to-one time with Heather
  • If a regular pattern appears, it can switch to cycle-guided

Heather's view

A simple list of clients showing who needs a look: low energy for a few days, no check-in, or a period that may be late. She reads what each woman chose to share and sends a personal note for the week. Until there's an app, a shared form or spreadsheet does this job.
