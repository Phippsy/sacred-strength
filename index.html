Stronger, not smaller.

Our mission: help women feel stronger by getting back in tune with their bodies, and training, resting and eating in step with how they actually feel through the month.

Sacred Strength is Heather's coaching practice in the making: personal training and yoga, by women, for women. This site holds what we've decided, what we're doing next, and everything we've learned so far.

The question we're answering

What do women in their 30s feel through the month, and what would help them listen to it?

How they feel

Physically and emotionally, across a typical month. In their own words.

What they track

Which apps or habits they use today, why, and what they do with it.

Have 8 to 10 conversations with women aged 30 to 40

Heather has relaxed, one-to-one chats with women from her network about how they feel through the month, what they've tried, and what would help. No pitching and nothing to sell. The aim is to listen and take notes.

What it involves
  1. 1
    Make a list10 to 12 women you know. Aim for a mix: regular and irregular cycles, on contraception, approaching perimenopause, gym-goers and not.
  2. 2
    Invite themA short, friendly message. There's one ready to copy in the guide.
  3. 3
    Talk30 to 45 minutes over coffee or video. Use the guide loosely and follow what she says.
  4. 4
    Jot notes straight afterHer words, how she feels, what surprised you. Ten minutes, no names.
  5. 5
    Review togetherSit down with Donal, look for patterns, and choose the next step.

It costs nothing

It doesn't need qualifications, insurance or an app, so it can start straight away.

It narrows the ideas

There are lots of good ideas. Hearing women tells us which one to back first.

It tests the core idea

If being listened to is what women value most, this is the first test of it.

It finds the first people
