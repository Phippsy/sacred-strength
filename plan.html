How we work

Test, learn, adjust

We treat each step as a small experiment. We say what we expect to happen, try the smallest thing that would tell us, and change course based on what we find.

This keeps costs low and stops us building things nobody wants. It also means the plan below will change, and that's how it's meant to work.

Risks

What could trip us up

Evidence

The science is thin

Research doesn't show that training by phase gets better results. We promise awareness and support, not performance gains.

Time

Coaching doesn't scale

One-to-one time is limited. Groups, written phase notes and a video library will matter as it grows.

Habit

People drift off

About 3% of people still use a free fitness app after 30 days. Heather's personal contact is the reason to come back.

Noise

A crowded space

New cycle-syncing apps appear every month. Standing out depends on Heather, her community and her local presence.

Rules

Health data and claims

Cycle data is sensitive. Collect little, keep it private, and avoid fertility or medical claims.

Reach

Not everyone has a cycle to follow

Hormonal contraception, irregular cycles and perimenopause. The feelings-guided approach is the answer to test.
